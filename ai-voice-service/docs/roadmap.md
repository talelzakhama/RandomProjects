# Roadmap and acceptance gates (DRAFT v0.1)

| Phase | Target | Deliverables | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0: Foundation | Week 1 | Repo/docs, provider comparison criteria, buyer interview guide | Scope and priorities approved |
| 1: Validation | Weeks 1–2 | 10 interviews, problem/risk findings, pilot shortlist | Evidence of willingness to pay |
| 2: Prototype | Weeks 2–4 | Mock call flow, provider sandbox, structured summary and test suite | Repeatable demos |
| 3: Hardening | Weeks 4–6 | Access control, consent/retention, monitoring, fallback, cost caps | Supervised pilot readiness |
| 4: Pilot | Weeks 6–10 | First business pilot, measured outcomes, support process | Business and technical viability |
| 5: Productize | Week 10+ | Reusable setup, onboarding, service agreements, integrations | Repeatable deployments |

Weeks are aspiration, not commitments. Go/no-go decisions belong to the owner.
